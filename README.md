# Hack4Freedom Kaduna 2025

📍 Kaduna, Nigeria  
🗓 September - October 2025  

Hack4Freedom Kaduna 2025 was the **inaugural Hack4Freedom hackathon** and the first women-focused Bitcoin hackathon of its kind.

This repository documents the program, people, projects, and outcomes from the Kaduna cohort and serves as a reference model for future Hack4Freedom hackathons across the Global South.

---

## About Hack4Freedom Kaduna

Hack4Freedom Kaduna was a **two-week hybrid hackathon** focused on building open-source freedom technology using **Bitcoin, Lightning, Nostr, and ecash**.

The program combined education, mentorship, and hands-on building in public GitHub repositories. Participants worked in teams to build tools addressing real local challenges, with an emphasis on learning, collaboration, and long-term impact.

---

## Program Structure

### Week 1 — Education & Ideation
- Bitcoin fundamentals and freedom tech context  
- Lightning, Nostr, and ecash workshops  
- Open-source workflows and GitHub onboarding  
- Team formation and problem discovery  
- Mentor-led technical sessions  

### Week 2 — Building & Mentorship
- Daily stand-ups and progress check-ins  
- Mentor office hours  
- Focused development sessions  
- Project iteration and testing  
- Demo Day preparation  

---

## Participants

- **20 female developers** participated in the program  
- Participants came from diverse technical backgrounds  
- Many were building with Bitcoin or Lightning for the first time  

Hack4Freedom Kaduna prioritized curiosity, commitment, and willingness to build in public over prior experience.

## Projects

Teams built **8 open-source prototypes** addressing real local challenges such as:
- Financial inclusion and savings  
- Lightning-based payments  
- Community coordination  
- Privacy-preserving financial tools  

All projects were developed in public repositories and documented for continued development.

👉 See the `/projects` directory for project summaries and links.

---

## Demo Day

The program concluded with an in-person Demo Day where teams presented:
- The problem they addressed  
- Their technical approach  
- Working prototypes  
- Next steps for continued development  

Demo Day emphasized learning, progress, and open-source contribution over polished pitches.

## Outcomes & Impact

Hack4Freedom Kaduna:
- Trained **20 female developers**
- Produced **8 open-source prototypes**
- Created a supportive, long-term builder community

Several projects continued development after the program, and participants remained engaged in the Bitcoin and open-source ecosystem.
